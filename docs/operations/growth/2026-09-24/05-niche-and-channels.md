## 0. Bottom line

1. **Paid social/search ads are closed to this topic.** Google, Meta, TikTok and Reddit ad policies prohibit promoting recreational/illicit drug use. Google also bans ads for "instructional content about ... using recreational drugs" (sec. 3a). A solo author cannot use the usual "run ads" approach. Reach has to come from niche-owned media, professional partners, Amazon, and search/AI answers.
2. **Outlets that draw attention in this niche each offer a named, specific free asset** (a guide, a course, a support line, a directory). mayaallan.com has one generic homepage form ("Stay Connected ... 1-2 emails per month"). It has **no email capture on /books, /blog, /events, /belief-inquiry** (live GET 18:23:00Z). /events says "No events are currently scheduled" (18:42:27Z).
3. **The Amazon listing is under-optimised, and the competition there is weak.** Paperback: **0 customer reviews**, BSR #3,949,951 in Books. It is shelved in *Inner Child / Emotional Self Help / Personal Transformation*, and the hardcover also in *Healthy Relationships*. That is not where comparable psychedelic books rank. The book still appears **6th** in Amazon Books search for "psilocybin integration". Most competing integration workbooks have 0-2 ratings. The category leader (Aixala, *Psychedelic Integration*) has 150 ratings and ranks #108,582.
4. **The biggest underused lever is the supply side of the legal programs.** Oregon has **383 licensed facilitators**, and only **22 of 35** approved centers are still operating. Q1-2026 clients fell **40% year on year**. Colorado has **34 licensed healing centers**, all of which stress integration, and facilitators must complete **continuing education**. 5. **Biggest demand hook for 2026-27: clinical psilocybin is going mainstream.** An executive order was signed on 2026-04-18. FDA priority vouchers followed on 2026-04-24. Compass expects to finish its COMP360 NDA in **Q4 2026** and to **launch in H1 2027**, if the FDA approves and the DEA reschedules. **No psychedelic legalization measure is on any November 2026 state ballot** (Ballotpedia live), so do not plan content around ballots.
