## 0. Thesis

The people who most need an integration guide are handed to someone else at the moment of need: the licensed facilitator, the service center, the retreat, the training program, the support line, the podcast host. Oregon law makes the gap explicit. The facilitator "must offer the client an opportunity to participate in an integration session. The client may, but need not, participate" (ORS 475A.360, F4). In Q2 2026, 545 of Oregon's 1,220 clients lived outside Oregon (525 elsewhere in the US and 20 abroad; F5). They fly home with nothing.

A pioneer does not chase those clients one search at a time. She becomes the default **take-home layer** that every program hands out. That layer is a free, co-branded, trackable integration kit: the existing server-side journal PDF, the 40 scenarios and the support lines. Paid tiers sit behind it: bulk ebook licences and a teaching casebook. One partner's yes reaches every client of that partner, every quarter. The partner universe is small enough to contact personally in 30 days: about 70 centers, about 560 licensed facilitators, 22 unique training programs and 7 active integration podcasts. It is named in public state data refreshed today.
