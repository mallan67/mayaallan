## 1. How a pioneer sees this market

A pioneer does not fight for the word "psilocybin" or buy ads (Google restricts them for this topic). A pioneer finds the moment nobody serves, names it, and stands there together with the gatekeepers who already touch it. For this book that moment is **the weeks after, back home**: the experience is over, the session (if there was one) is over, and the questions start.
