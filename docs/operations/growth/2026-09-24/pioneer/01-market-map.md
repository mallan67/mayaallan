# Pioneer Market Map: Psilocybin Integration, 2026-09-24

**Scope.** Who needs psilocybin-integration help in 2026, how many of them there are, where they are, what they search for, what they pay, who already serves them, and which gaps a first mover could own. The subject is *The Psilocybin Integration Guide* by Maya Allan (ebook sold on the site; paperback and hardcover through retailers; audiobook in production) and www.mayaallan.com.

**How this was built.** Every number below comes from a live public source read on **2026-09-24 between 18:24Z and 18:44Z** (UTC). Each source has its URL, publication/update date and read time in §9. Nothing comes from memory, local files or earlier reports. When a live source could not be reached, the item is marked **UNVERIFIED**. When a number comes from a third party rather than the official program, it is marked **(secondary)**.

**Positioning guardrail.** The live /books page describes the book as "non-clinical, educational frameworks for reflection, integration, and self-inquiry". Every segment below is approached with that framing: education and reflection, never treatment, dosing or access.

## 0. How a pioneer would read this market (five theses)

1. **Oregon's paying client is a 45+, higher-income traveler who goes home.** In Q2 2026, **71%** of Oregon clients who gave a county came from outside Oregon. **59%** were 45 or older, and **64%** of those who reported income earned $95,376 or more a year. A session costs about $1,500–3,500 (secondary). By law the integration session only has to be *offered*, and the client may decline it (ORS 475A.360). Most of these clients therefore do their integration at home, where no licensed program exists. **That gap between the session and home is the wedge.**
2. **The biggest group is unregulated and very large.** In 2024, **7.89 million** Americans aged 12+ used psilocybin in the past year (NSDUH 2024, the first year it was measured directly). RAND's September 2025 survey found **about 11 million adults**, **69%** of whom microdosed at least once. The official programs serve fewer than 10,000 people a year, less than 0.1% of this group.
3. **People who struggle afterwards help themselves by reading and journaling.** In a 608-person study, the most common individual coping strategies were meditation and prayer, "followed by self-educational activities such as reading and journaling" (Robinson et al. 2024). That describes a book plus the site's free journal and reflection tools.
4. **Clinical psilocybin is coming soon.** Compass Pathways told the SEC it expects to finish its rolling FDA new-drug application (NDA) for COMP360 in treatment-resistant depression (TRD) in Q4 2026, with **launch expected in H1 2027** if approved. New Mexico's medical program is scheduled to launch at the **end of 2026**. The first non-clinical integration companions that are ready when these open will set the reference.
5. **B2B is where the leads concentrate.** Oregon has **383** licensed facilitators, Colorado has **118** approved facilitator licenses plus **501** people holding facilitator-training licenses, and there are about **31** active training programs across the two states. A casebook of 40 real scenarios is teaching material. One program adoption means a new cohort every term.
